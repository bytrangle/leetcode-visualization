Product Requirements Document

Leet Years

status: approved

A web app that replays a LeetCode user's practice history as an animated, difficulty-colored heatmap. Canonical source: devpost/prd.md.

What changed from scope: "the replay colors the grid in" is now pinned down — one year shown at a time with a whole-grid card flip between years; colors are a continuous green→yellow→red blend keyed to average difficulty (hard = 2× medium), not three buckets; the popup's "2 seconds or something" became 0.5s; and the undefined playback/error behaviors now have exact answers.

The journey

The core journey

One page, one loop. The visitor lands, enters a public LeetCode username, and watches their practice history replay year by year. That's the whole product.

  1. Land on the title, welcome line, input, and a Start button.
  2. Type a username, press Start.
  3. The landing gives way to a ~365-cell light-grey grid; the year label shows the earliest year with activity.
  4. Cells fill in date order, each a vertical card flip, colored by that day's average difficulty.
  5. A popup lists each day's problems with difficulty dots for ~0.5s, then the next day fills.
  6. When the year is done, the whole grid flips to the next year — label advances, cells reset grey.
  7. Repeat until the final year; then the replay ends (Play restarts, if controls are shown).

Landing & Lookup

The visitor enters a public LeetCode username and presses Start. The app reads that user's practice calendar.

  • Landing shows title, welcome line, input, and Start.
  • Submitting a valid username replaces the landing with a ~365-cell light-grey grid; label = earliest year with data.

Data source is a 4-spec concern (see scope.md > Notes); the PRD only defines what the visitor experiences.

Replay & Popup

Cells fill in date order, earliest active day first, each via a vertical card flip. Days with no solved problems stay light grey and are skipped.

Color  easy = 1medium = 2hard = 4 — a day's color is the continuous blend at its average difficulty score (hard counts twice medium). A popup beside each newly-filled cell lists its problem titles with per-problem difficulty dots, auto-dismissing after ~0.5s.

  • Active days fill in date order, each a card flip, with the blended average-difficulty color.
  • A popup beside each new cell lists titles + difficulty dots, clearing after ~0.5s.
  • Hovering a filled cell re-shows its popup.
  • No-data days remain grey and are skipped.

Year transition & end

After a year's last active day, the whole grid does one vertical card flip — the same flip as individual cells. The year label advances, all cells reset to light grey, and the replay resumes. It continues through the final year with data, then stops.

  • After a year's last active day, the whole grid card-flips to the next year (label updates, cells reset grey) and continues.
  • The replay ends after the final year's last active day.

Controls (conditional)

Shown only when the replay lasts more than ~20s (≈ over 40 active days, estimated as active days × 0.5s). One toggle button (play ↔ pause; after the end, it restarts from the earliest year). Above it, a seek bar — a thin bar with a round handle; dragging jumps straight to the state at that point (no scrub animation) and resumes from there.

  • Controls appear only for histories longer than ~20s.
  • Play/pause toggles; play after end restarts.
  • Seek bar jumps to a point and resumes from there.

No speed control, no skip-year button — the seek bar covers both.

Empty & error states

In every failure case the heatmap is not shown, and the input stays so the visitor can correct or retry.

  • Nonexistent username → "Sorry, this username doesn't exist."
  • Existing user, zero solves → "Sorry, this user hasn't solved any problems yet."
  • Data load failure → "Sorry, we couldn't load the data right now — please try again."

Decisions & boundary

Product decisions (learner's): name "Leet Years"; one-year-per-view with a whole-grid card flip; continuous color blend (rejected three buckets and orange-as-medium); 0.5s popup with hover re-inspect; adaptive controls past ~20s; minimal play/pause + seek that jumps; friendly "Sorry…" errors; minimal-but-friendly look.

Building now: the loop above and nothing else.

Deferred: speed control, skip-year buttons, persistence, sharing — none are in the proof of concept.

Non-goals: accounts, login, storage, writing to LeetCode, social features, or any metric beyond difficulty-colored presence.
